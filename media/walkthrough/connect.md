# Connect two chats

On the Tower, **drag one card onto another**. That's a channel: the second chat now hears what the
first one does.

Click the line between them to choose how much it sends:

| | What goes over |
|---|---|
| **Ping** | one line per ask (the default) |
| **Brief** | what was asked, done and still open, with files and PRs |
| **Full** | the words themselves |

You can also pause a channel, make it two-way, or close it.
