# The Tower

Every chat you have had in this folder with **Claude Code**, **Codex** or **Gemini CLI**, as a card:
what it is doing, how full its context is, and which other chats it shares with.

- **Arrange** them by channel, time, agent or folder.
- **Click a card** to open the chat where it lives.
- **New task** starts a fresh chat from an old one's record.

Crosstalk reads the session files your agents already keep on this computer. It runs no AI of its own
and never reads a login.
