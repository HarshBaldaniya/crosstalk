Recap the meeting: {{meeting}}.

1. Find it with the meeting tools; read its notes, and the transcript for anything the notes leave out.
2. **Decisions:** what was agreed, one line each.
3. **Action items:** what, who, by when; only owners named in the meeting.
4. **Open questions:** what's still undecided and who has to answer.
5. Report what people said in your own words; quote only if asked. Nothing that wasn't said.
