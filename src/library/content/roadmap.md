---
name: roadmap
title: Plan a roadmap
pack: product
description: Order the work into now, next and later, with the reason for each choice.
args: items = The ideas, requests or goals to order (default: open items from recent chats); horizon = How far ahead (default: one quarter)
triggers: roadmap, prioritize, what should we do next, plan the quarter, now next later, backlog
---
Plan {{horizon}} from: {{items}}.

1. **List the candidates,** merging duplicates. For open work, `crosstalk_flightlog` shows what recent chats left open.
2. **For each, one line:** the outcome it brings, rough effort (days, weeks, months), and confidence that it works.
3. **Order them:** Now (started or next up, fits this horizon), Next (ready once Now lands), Later (worth doing, not yet). Put things that unblock others earlier. Leave out what doesn't serve a goal, and say so.
4. **For each Now item:** what done looks like, and the first step.
5. **Risks:** dependencies, people, unknowns that could reorder this.

Plain words. A table for the three columns, then three lines on what you'd say no to and why.
