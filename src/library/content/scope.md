Scope: {{idea}}, for {{users}}.

Write in plain words, no jargon. If who it's for or what success means is unclear, ask up to three short questions first.

1. **The problem:** what the person can't do today, and what it costs them. One short paragraph.
2. **Who:** the main user, and anyone else affected.
3. **The smallest useful version:** what they can do with it on day one, as three to five "They can ..." lines.
4. **Left out on purpose:** what waits for later, and why.
5. **How we'll know it worked:** one or two things to measure, with a target.
6. **Open questions and risks:** what could make this the wrong thing to build.

End with: the one decision someone needs to make before work starts.
