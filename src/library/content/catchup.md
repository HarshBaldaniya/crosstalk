Catch up on the other chats in this folder, {{since}}.

1. `crosstalk_flightlog` with no chat lists recent chats; take the ones in this folder updated in that time, other than this one.
2. For each, read its newest asks (chat, last: 10).
3. **Report per chat:** what it set out to do, what it finished, what's open, and the files it changed.
4. **Then what matters to this chat:** files both of you changed (possible conflicts), decisions that change your plan, and anything you should pick up. Keep it short.
