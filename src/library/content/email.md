---
name: email
title: Draft an email
pack: connected
needs: gmail
description: Draft a clear email (follow-up, update, reply or request) and save it as a draft; you send it.
args: purpose* = What the email is for; to = Who it's to (default: whoever the thread or the person names)
triggers: write an email, draft an email, follow up email, reply to this email, email the client
---
Draft an email to {{to}}: {{purpose}}.

1. If replying, read the thread first with the mail tools.
2. **Subject:** specific, under 60 characters ("Invoice 1042: corrected total").
3. **Body:** the point in the first line; context in two or three short sentences; one clear ask with a date if there is one; a short sign-off. No filler openings or closings.
4. Facts only from the thread, the session logs or what the person said. Never invent numbers, dates or promises.
5. Save it as a **draft** and show it. Never send; the person sends.
