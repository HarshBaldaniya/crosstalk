name: research
title: Make sense of feedback
pack: product
description: Turn interviews, feedback or support messages into themes, real quotes and what to do next.
args: source* = The notes, messages or file to read; question = What you want to learn (default: what users struggle with)
triggers: user research, synthesize feedback, interview notes, customer feedback, what are users saying, survey results
---
Read {{source}} to answer: {{question}}.

1. **Read everything once** before grouping. Note who said what (role, not name, unless names are already public).
2. **Themes:** group what was said into three to six themes. For each: how many people raised it, one or two short quotes in their own words, and how strongly it was felt.
3. **Separate** what people did or experienced (strong evidence) from what they asked for (weaker; a request is a guess at a solution).
4. **Surprises:** anything that contradicts what the team believed.
5. **What to do next:** for the top themes, one action each, from "fix now" to "watch for more evidence".

Never invent a quote or a count. If the material is too thin to say something, say so.
